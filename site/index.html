Peer allocation of credit

The people doing the work decide who gets paid.

Bounties pay for work you can specify in advance. Communities run on work you cannot specify in advance: hosting, moderating, mentoring, showing up. FairShare lets a group fund that work together, and the contract settles every payout onchain, with each one explainable giver by giver.
