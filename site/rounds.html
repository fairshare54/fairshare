Round 3 is the reputation round: the first round settled under the standing rule. Eleven members allocated and one, Tunde, deliberately submitted nothing, to prove the penalty live. Payouts add to 4.999 NEAR, 5 yocto of dust stays in the contract. Ada was capped at exactly 1.250 NEAR. Settle: 7ZbAQganvPJKGHsQkUGYXE56tdvqLm9UM1CGRwcCkMWG · Close: C3nXKn8MKioieU5wW6AZGEYEqQ7CDBVebttaxNSNEYcJ · Funding proposal #7: LiZkXGB2phs1xhqZbxuFmFctYuHTAEPh8DLYGWowvid

The funding proposal taught the project something real. It failed twice before it passed: the DAO looks rich on its total balance, but Sputnik stores every proposal onchain, so the DAO must keep about 4.74 NEAR staked for its own storage, and executing a proposal also returns the 1 NEAR bond. A 5 NEAR envelope therefore needs the DAO at about 11.5 NEAR total, not 5. The DAO was topped up to 11.5 NEAR and the seventh vote passed. The full story, with the failed votes, is in the repo runbook.

The gates, proved live

Before the settle, Ada, standing 100, tried to close the round early. The call was rejected: closing early needs standing 150. After the settle, Tunde, standing 95, tried to propose a round. The call was rejected: proposing needs standing 100. No round 4 was created. Both rejections are failed receipts onchain; the contract code is built to abort on a failed check, so the receipt carries no message text, only the failed call and the unchanged state.

The DAO and governance

Membership in FairShare is a Sputnik DAO role, read live across contracts. The contract mirrors the DAO council into its member set with sync_dao_members, and get_dao_role checks one account live against the DAO policy. The DAO is the source of truth. The local member set is a synced mirror.

DAO: fairshare-dao.testnet · Name: fairshare · Council: 12 members, the 6 demo members plus the 6 round 2 crew · Policy: 1 of 2 vote to pass, 1 NEAR proposal bond, 7 day proposal period

DAO deploy: 23KcWfmQD2cu6nBJkx1yAoWk8istBR75omCzXVfZUDnv · DAO init: DV39wdiRmhgLAwZ2TCtZncR8RVMhQQ8iPy7jHd2YyYug

  1. Join. A new member joins the DAO council by proposal. The council votes, and the proposal executes. Sputnik decides approval by proposal kind and role, never by amount.
  2. Sync. Anyone calls sync_dao_members on the round contract. It reads the DAO get_policy live and mirrors the council into the member set.
  3. Fund. The DAO funds a round by proposing a FunctionCall to create_round with the envelope attached. The council votes, the proposal executes, and the envelope sits in escrow in the contract.
  4. Allocate and settle. Members allocate, the owner closes the round early or the 7 day deadline passes, and anyone calls settle_round. The contract pays each member from the envelope.

One fact worth knowing: the live testnet factory only creates DAOs as its own subaccounts, so it cannot create the top level name fairshare-dao.testnet. The real Sputnik v2 contract was deployed directly to that account instead, with the wasm read onchain from genesis.sputnikv2.testnet.

Trezu manages this DAO

Trezu is the treasury app for Sputnik DAOs. This DAO is a standard Sputnik DAO, so it is the kind of DAO Trezu manages: council members propose, vote and execute through it, and the contract reads membership live from it.

The honest limit: Trezu production is mainnet only, so there is no live Trezu testnet payout, and none is claimed here. What was verified is the local testnet view: the Trezu frontend run locally with the mock backend from the Trezu repo, opening fairshare-dao.testnet, shows the real onchain data. Requests, Executed tab: all 8 proposals, each Executed, including the round 2 and round 3 funding proposals. Members: all 12 council members. Settings, Voting: 6 of 12 threshold, 7 day vote duration. Assets and recent transactions stay empty, because there is no indexer for testnet DAOs. The exact steps and screenshots are in the repo, TREZU.md.

Check it yourself

Nothing on this page asks you to trust it. Read the contract state with plain view calls:

  1. get_params on fairshare-contract.testnet returns 100 points per member, at least 3 recipients, cap 2500 basis points, discount 5000 basis points, round 604800000000000 nanoseconds, standing starts at 100, and the reputation rule: +2 per distinct giver, capped at +10 a round, minus 5 for no allocation, floor 50, cap 200, propose at 100, close early at 150.
  2. get_dao_info returns the DAO id, the member role, the member count, and the last sync time.
  3. get_round with round id 1 returns the 5 NEAR envelope, closed true, settled true, total payout 4999999999999999999999997 yocto.
  4. get_payouts with round id 1 returns all six payouts. explain_payout with round id 1 and any member returns their score broken down by giver.
  5. get_policy on fairshare-dao.testnet shows the council role with 12 members, the 6 demo members plus the 6 round 2 crew.
