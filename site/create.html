Your own FairShare group

Create your DAO

Start a FairShare group for the people you work with. You create the group, you get an invite link, your members join, and the group allocates its own money, onchain, with every payout explainable. No technical person needed.

Connect your testnet wallet above to create a group. Nothing moves without your signature.

How it works, in three steps

  1. Create. Your wallet signs four steps: your DAO is created, the group is listed in the directory, your group's contract is deployed, and your membership is synced. Each step checks the chain first, so if you reload halfway, you continue where you stopped.
  2. Invite with your link. You get an invite link. Members open it, connect, and join through a DAO proposal your council approves.
  3. Allocate your 100 points. In an open round, every member gives 100 points to at least 3 others, with a reason each. The contract settles the payouts onchain.

What it costs

Creating a group is free on testnet in the sense that no fee is charged. The NEAR below is not a fee: it funds your group's own accounts, and most of it stays there.

  • About 10.5 NEAR for your group's DAO. About 5.4 NEAR of that stays locked as the DAO's storage, and about 5.1 NEAR stays in the DAO treasury, which funds your first 5 NEAR round.
  • About 5.2 NEAR for your group's contract. It stays locked in the contract's account as storage for the contract code.
  • 1 NEAR to register the group. Almost all of it is refunded; only the small storage cost of the directory record is kept.
  • 1 NEAR per proposal bond. The bond is returned when the council approves the proposal.

In total, have about 17.7 testnet NEAR in your wallet before you start. Testnet NEAR is free from the NEAR faucet. Rounds last 7 days; the contract fixes that for now. There are no mainnet parts here: this whole flow is testnet only.
